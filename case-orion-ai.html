> The Challenge

Running a company means repeating the same operational work every day: standups, reminders, research, drafting, admin. Paid tools like Slack solve pieces of it — but at a recurring cost, and they never actually do the work.

> The Solution

Built Orion AI — an AI assistant that operates a complete virtual office inside Discord:

  • Server admin — manages roles, channels and housekeeping automatically
  • Memory — persistent context across conversations
  • Web research — pulls current information on demand
  • Scheduled standups & reminders — runs on a timer, no one has to prompt it
  • Content drafting — writes and formats drafts on command
> Results
  • Replaced paid Slack — monthly subscription eliminated
  • Standups, reminders and research now run on schedule, unattended
  • One assistant replaces a stack of disconnected tools
