It started with a button. I was a kid in Dhaka, and there was a computer in the house that nobody could explain to me. So I pressed things until I understood them. That habit, pressing until something works or breaks, has carried every job I have had since.

At 21 I co-founded Best Tech Center with a soldering iron and a Facebook page. No office, no staff, no plan beyond fixing what people brought in. We grew it to 50,00,000 BDT in annual revenue, one repaired machine at a time. I learned that customer trust is not a marketing problem. It is a delivery problem. Fix the machine, and the customer comes back.

"Technology only matters when it solves real human problems."

In 2022 I moved to Japan with no Japanese. Two years at Asahi University learning the language, then Nippon Omotenashi College for Business IT. Japan changed how I work. Kaizen, the slow accumulation of small improvements, is now the way I write code and the way I run a company.

Today I run Brainsroot Inc. from Maebashi, building websites, web apps and desktop tools for clients in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. I do the strategy, the code, the launch and the support. When a client's law firm shows up correctly in ChatGPT because of the schema markup I wrote, that is the job done right.

I use AI the way I use every other tool: to remove the manual. My own company runs on Orion AI, a Discord assistant I built that handles standups, reminders and research on a schedule. It replaced a paid Slack subscription. I teach the teams I work with to do the same, not because AI is fashionable, but because it frees people for work that needs a human.

I also founded the International Society of Artists (ISA), which raised money for art scholarships. Creativity should be funded, not filtered. That was the whole idea.

"The best work happens when you stop choosing between being a developer and being a founder, and start being both."

I am still learning. Right now it is AI agents, API architecture and cloud infrastructure. I do not know what comes next. I know it will start with something I do not understand, and I will take it apart until I do.
