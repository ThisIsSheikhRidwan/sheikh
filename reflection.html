My work with computers started in Dhaka. At Dhaka Collegiate School (3.78 GPA) and St. Gregory's High School & College (3.92 GPA, Vice President of Business Club), I spent my early years doing hardware diagnostics and taking machines apart to understand how they worked.

At 21, I co-founded Best Tech Center BD with hardware diagnostic tools and a Facebook page. We built a customer-focused tech service business that grew to 50,00,000 BDT in annual revenue. Fixing hardware and software for hundreds of clients taught me a core lesson: customer trust is earned by delivering reliability every single time.

Alongside tech support, I expanded into product design and branding at Prayas Group and founded the International Society of Artists (ISA). We built a global platform for young artists and secured funding for youth scholarships, proving that clear execution can connect creative talent across international borders.

"Technology only matters when it solves real human problems."

In 2022, I relocated to Japan. I completed two years of intensive Japanese Language & Literature at Asahi University, achieving business fluency (JLPT N2), before studying Business Information & Technology at Nippon Omotenashi College in Maebashi. Japan taught me kaizen (the habit of continuous small improvements), which governs how I structure code and run projects.

While studying in Japan, I also operated as a P2P Crypto Trader on Binance, executing 516 USDT transactions (277 settled, largest single execution at ¥903,000). Managing liquidity and compliance in fast-paced markets sharpened my risk management and decision-making under operational pressure.

Today, I am the Founder & CEO of Brainsroot Inc., leading digital transformation for businesses across the US, Canada, UK, and Australia. I build full-stack web applications, SaaS platforms, desktop software, and AI-powered automation workflows. We build internal tools like Orion AI (a Discord virtual office assistant that replaced paid Slack) and implement AI search optimization (schema markup) so client sites surface accurately in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers.

"The best work happens when you stop choosing between being a developer and being a founder, and start being both."

My daily stack centers on Node.js, TypeScript, REST APIs, n8n automation, AI agent architectures, and cloud deployments. I build systems, craft brand identities, and solve problems end-to-end.
