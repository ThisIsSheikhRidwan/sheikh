> The Challenge

Enlight Lawyers, a Queensland-based law firm, needed a complete digital transformation: a professional brand identity, a modern website that converts visitors into clients, and a strong SEO foundation to compete in the legal market.

Project scope: brand identity & logo design · custom legal website · SEO & keyword research · AI response optimization · client communication templates

> The Approach
  • 01 · Brand Identity: professional visual identity: logo, color palette, typography and brand guidelines reflecting the firm's values and expertise
  • 02 · Website & SEO: comprehensive legal-industry SEO and keyword research; a website optimized for both Google rankings and AI search visibility
  • 03 · AI Optimization: content structure and schema markup tuned for AI response generation, so the firm appears accurately in ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI search tools
> Results
  • Complete: full brand identity and digital presence
  • SEO: comprehensive legal keyword research
  • AI-Ready: firm surfaces accurately in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, verified live
